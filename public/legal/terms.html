2. What you are buying

A licence that allows a stated number of computers to back up to one BackupMate server for a stated period, normally one year. The licence is a key that you install on that server. It is issued for that server and does not work on another.

A paid licence also includes, for as long as it is in force: the updates we release, and support as described in section 6.

You are not buying storage, hardware, or a service that we run. BackupMate runs on your equipment, and your backups are stored on your equipment.

Prices are as shown on the pricing page on the day you order, or as quoted to you in writing. Prices for customers in India are in Indian rupees and exclude GST, which is added at the applicable rate and shown on your invoice. Prices for customers elsewhere are in US dollars; any taxes or duties due in your country are your responsibility unless the invoice says otherwise.

Payment is due in full before a licence is issued. We issue an invoice for every purchase.

4. Delivery

The licence is delivered by email, normally within one working day of payment being received. Nothing physical is shipped.

5. Term, renewal and expiry

A licence runs from its start date to the expiry date stated in it. It does not renew automatically and we do not take repeat payments without a fresh order from you. We will normally remind you before it expires.

When a licence expires it continues to be honoured for 14 days. After that your server returns to the free plan: up to 3 computers continue to back up, and the others stop taking new backups until a licence is installed. Your existing backups are not deleted and restoring them is never restricted, whether or not a licence is in force.

If you need more computers during the term, you may buy a larger licence and pay the difference for the remaining period.

6. Support

Support covers installing, configuring and using BackupMate and recovering your files with it. It does not cover repairing your computers, network or other software, although we will usually tell you what we think is wrong.

You are responsible for: choosing what is backed up; keeping the server running and with enough disk space; keeping your encryption passphrase safe; acting on the alerts BackupMate sends; testing from time to time that you can restore; and keeping a second copy of anything you cannot afford to lose.
